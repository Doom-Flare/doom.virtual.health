## What to capture

A screenshot that says both "what this is" and "it is running". Suggested approach:

1. Enter a 1.21.5 – 26.3 world (pick the matching variant from the mapping table), install `doom.virtual.health/`, `/reload`;
2. Summon a mob with virtual health (write a health value vanilla could never produce, so it is obvious at a glance this is not vanilla health):

   ```
   /summon minecraft:zombie ~ ~ ~ {data:{dvh:{max_health:40d, health:40d, on_death:"say died"}}, NoAI:1b}
   ```

3. Wait for the health bar to appear at its feet, hit it twice (the bar must move but the mob must not die), then press F2. The screenshot must show both the mob and the health bar.
